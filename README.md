# Retail Business Intelligence Platform

## Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales data to identify sales trends, profitability,
customer behavior, product performance, and regional performance.

## Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance
- Identify top-performing categories and products
- Analyze regional sales performance
- Identify profitable and loss-making sub-categories
- Analyze customer segments
- Calculate important business KPIs
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard

## Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- MySQL
- SQL
- Power BI

## Key Business Insights
-Total sales are approximately 13M, while total profit is approximately 1.47M.
-Technology is the highest-selling category, contributing approximately 37.5% of total sales.
-Consumer is the largest customer segment, contributing approximately 7M in sales.
-Among the regions displayed in the Top 5 analysis, Central has the highest total sales.
-Copiers generate the highest profit among the sub-categories displayed in the dashboard.
-The dashboard's Top 10 analysis highlights the highest-selling products based on total sales.
-Sales and profit show an overall increasing trend across the years shown in the dashboard.
